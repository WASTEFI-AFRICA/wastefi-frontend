# Code of Conduct

WasteFi is built in the open by people in many countries, most of them volunteers. This
code of conduct applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any other
space where you speak for the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or are
  working in a second language.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Say what you would change and why.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and say so when you disagree instead of going quiet.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Do not post other people's private information, and do not post tokens, passwords or
  private keys.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about someone's race, ethnicity, nationality,
  gender, sexuality, disability, age, religion or background.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Spam, and pull requests made to trick maintainers, such as hiding code or forging
  commits. Those are removed and blocked.

## Reporting

To report a problem, email **umubianny9@gmail.com** with what happened and a link if there
is one. Reports are read by the project maintainers and handled privately. Please do not
report conduct problems in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities go through the
private reporting link in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), not this address.

## What happens next

Maintainers may warn, edit or remove comments, close a pull request or issue, or restrict
someone from the repository. We will tell the person who reported what we decided where
we can, and we will not name them to the person reported.
